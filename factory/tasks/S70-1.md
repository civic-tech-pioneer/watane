## Goal
When a dial fails, net's `Unreachable` cause names only what the participant has not yet chosen.
Today, with a relay chosen and the DHT off (or the DHT on and no relay), the cause reads as if both
were tried; S68's notes asked that it say what is missing. This is the one cause text of
`no_path_cause` in net/src/reach.rs, nothing else.

## Acceptance
- AC1 (unit, net/src/reach.rs tests) `no_path_cause(relays, dht)` gives exactly:
  - `(false, false)`: `no direct path; choose a relay or turn on the DHT` (unchanged);
  - `(true, false)`: `no direct path and no answer through the relay; turn on the DHT`;
  - `(false, true)`: `no direct path and no answer through the DHT; choose a relay`;
  - `(true, true)`: `the machine did not answer at any known address, by the relay or by the DHT` (unchanged).
- AC2 net/TECHNICAL.md (5.10, near "no direct path; choose a relay or turn on the DHT") and NET-F-28
  in net/FUNCTIONAL.md if it quotes the text list the four causes.
- AC3 `cargo test -p watane-net` passes, its run time stays near its current ~39 s (no new
  integration test; the unit test is the proof), clippy and fmt clean.
