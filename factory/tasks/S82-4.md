## Goal
When Desk calls `api.open-page` with an installed mod's local name, the app opens that mod's window, or
focuses it if it is open, as the Library's Open does. While a consent view is open it opens nothing
(the call is dropped, not queued), so a manager can never put a window over a consent (host 18 Q31 b).
