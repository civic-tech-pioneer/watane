## Goal
A WIT syntax error in a mod's `wit/` gives its file, line and column. Today
`pkg/src/world.rs` (`read`, the `map.parse()` and `push_groups` errors near :222 and :249,
and the formatting at :258) turns wit-parser's error into `e.to_string()`, which drops the
`--> <path>:<line>:<col>` location that wit-parser renders only through its `SourceMap`. After
this task the `Rule::Wit` violation's message carries wit-parser's rendered location (the
highlighted form wit-parser produces from the source map, e.g. `SourceMap::render_location` /
the error's highlighted rendering in wit-parser 0.254.2; read the crate source in
`~/.cargo/registry` to find the exact call) for parse errors and, where wit-parser gives one, for
resolve errors. The violation's `file` is the file that the location names (relative,
`wit/...`), as today's lookup at :259 tries to do.
