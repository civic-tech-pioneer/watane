## Goal
A holder `ingest` with `Reason::Kept` that ends with nothing stored for the channel (no head: a
gap, a bad entry, any log failure after the record was marked) leaves no `kept` record behind,
since nothing is kept. Today `Dist::ingest` (dist/src/holder.rs) marks the record before
`log.ingest` and keeps it when the log stores nothing. Product owner decision of S64.

## Acceptance
- AC1 A keyless home with no record for channel C: `ingest(C, entries starting at seq 3, Kept)`
  gives `Failed(Gap ..)`; `follows.json` has no record for C afterwards, and `heads(None)` does not
  list C.
- AC2 Same with an entry list whose first entry has a changed byte (log refuses it): no record for C.
- AC3 A record for C that existed before the call (kept, or followed) is left exactly as it was
  when such an ingest fails: only a mark that this call added is taken back.
- AC4 A successful `Kept` ingest still writes the `kept` record (existing tests).
- AC5 dist/TECHNICAL.md 5.12 (the ingest steps near "A gap with nothing stored and no head") says
  the kept record added by the call is removed when nothing is stored.
- AC6 `cargo test -p watane-dist` passes; clippy and fmt clean.
