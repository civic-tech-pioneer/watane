## Goal
Eve installs Dave's `stats` from his link. The app fetches its missing provider Carol's
`ratings` with `dist.fetch_for` (through the base Dave's listing names), installs it first, and
then follows Carol. When that fetch fails, the app searches Eve's indexes and followed channels
for a released `ratings` of another publisher that offers every interface `stats` uses, asks Eve
to pick one (the `pick` request of S37-1), adds a bind for it, and the install consent shows the
bind; the picked publisher is followed after the install. With no fit, or a deny, nothing is
installed. The main window shows where a mod was made from and the mods made from it.
