## Goal
A `Log` can be opened with no identity: it reads, verifies and stores other channels exactly as a
keyed `Log` does, never signs, and never creates a key. A keyed `Log` whose identity signed nothing
can drop it and become keyless. `Log::identity()` becomes `Option<PublicKey>`; a keyed `Log`
behaves as before. Stage L6 of log/TECHNICAL.md 9.

  entries 0..=2 of another author (made in the test with the oracle) ingest and read back byte-equal.

  included only once it has an entry.
- AC6 every L1–L5 test still passes, with `identity()` unwrapped where it is read.
