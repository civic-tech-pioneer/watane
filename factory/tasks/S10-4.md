## Goal
A mod page in the app is a sealed box: the `hostile` fixture, opened from the launcher, reaches
no app or plugin command, opens no window, never leaves its origin, gets fresh web storage in
each window, and its window closes when Alice disables the mod. The consent window stays above
mod windows. `tests/gui_isolation.rs` proves S5 to S8 on the real app against the fake catalog
and the fixture page server.

## Acceptance
All in `tests/gui_isolation.rs` (`#![cfg(feature = "driver")]`), with `Pages::start` over
`tests/fixtures/`, a `Listener`, and `WATANE_APP_FAKE` naming the mod `hostile` with page
`<Pages.url("hostile")>?listener=<Listener.port>&api=<Pages.port>`.
- AC1 (S5) With the `hostile` window open, a driver `ask` opens `consent`; driver `windows`
  shows `consent` with `alwaysOnTop: true`, `visible: true`, `focused: true`, and
  `mod-hostile` with `alwaysOnTop: false`. ("visible on all spaces" has no driver field: check
  `.visible_on_all_workspaces(true)` in `views.rs` by a text search in the test, and say so.)
- AC2 (S6) After the hostile page reports `done` and `navTried` (an `eval` poll loop, 30 s
  bound): every attack has outcome `refused`; driver `log` has no command with a label starting
  `mod-`; `blocked` has at least one `navigation` and one `new_window` entry for `mod-hostile`,
  and one `download` entry if WebKit reaches the download hook (record which in `NOTES.md`);
  `windows` has no label other than `main` and `mod-hostile`; the `mod-hostile` URL still has
  the origin `http://hostile.localhost:<port>`; `Listener.count()` is 0; the file tree of
  `<home>/mods/` is unchanged (absent or empty, as before).
- AC3 (S7) First window: `storageBefore` is `null`. Close `mod-hostile`, open it again from the
  launcher: `storageBefore` is `null` again.
- AC4 (S8) With `mod-hostile` open, clicking `button.disable` of its launcher entry makes the
  window disappear from `windows`; `mods_list` shows it `enabled: false`.
- AC5 `cargo test -p watane-app --features driver` (all tests, including S10-3's), clippy
  `--all-targets --features driver -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check` pass.
