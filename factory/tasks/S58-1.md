## Goal

A person can remove a toolset from the app's agents view, not only end a token
(host stage 4 is then complete: `Host::remove_toolset` already exists at
host/src/tools.rs:195 and host/tests/catalog.rs:1000 covers it). And six texts
read as names and causes, not keys and transport text: sign/install consents put
the name first, a wrong-channel follow names the parties, a mod card's refresh
error names the channel, a non-JSON `/api/fetch` body keeps its cause, a new home
shows the "add an index" hint alone, and saved toolsets show tool names that the
app's Rust derives, not the page's JS. App only, plus one sentence in
host/TECHNICAL.md.

## Acceptance

`host.remove_toolset`; an unknown name gives `no_toolset` (the host's code,
through the existing `From<HostError>`). Each saved toolset row in the agents
view has a `Remove` button (class `remove-toolset`) that calls it; the row and
