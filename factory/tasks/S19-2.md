## Goal
`log` stages L3 and L4: the SQLite channel store behind `Log` (append, ingest, entries, head,
status) refuses a corrupt row and records a forked chain as a permanent proof; an identity can be
exported as a 108-byte passphrase backup and restored into another directory, after which the own
channel takes no append until the caller confirms its own head.

## Acceptance
- AC1 (S15, S19) `append.rs`: three appends give seq 0..2, prev = BLAKE3 of the previous bytes,
  each signature verifies with `ed25519_compact` over the oracle's `cbor::signed`; a 33-byte kind,
  an upper-case kind and a 65 537-byte body fail (`Kind`, `Kind`, `BodyTooLarge`) and the head is unchanged.
- AC2 (S20–S25) `ingest.rs`: shuffled 0..4 stored, head 4; head 1 + entries 3,4 -> nothing stored,
  gap 3; a second valid entry 1 -> `Compromised` with both entries, stored 1 unchanged; entry 2
  with a wrong prev -> `Compromised(entry 1, that entry 2)`; after a fork the next valid entry is
  not stored and a reopen keeps the same proof; a compromised own channel refuses `append` with
  `Compromised`; an entry of another author -> `AuthorMismatch` and nothing written.
- AC3 (S26) `corrupt.rs`: the test drops `entries_no_update` with its own `rusqlite::Connection`,
  flips one byte of `raw`; `head`, `entries`, `status` fail with `CorruptStore` naming author and seq.
- AC4 (S27) `concurrency.rs`: two `Log`s on one dir, two threads, 50 appends each -> seq 0..99, every
  prev link valid (checked with `blake3` in the test).
- AC5 (S28) `model.rs`: proptest (256 cases, fixed seed) against the test's own `model::Chain`.
- AC6 (S7–S13) `backup.rs`: the 4.9 golden vector restores to the RFC 8032 key; export then restore
  in a fresh dir gives the same key, also after reopen; the export decrypts with `argon2` +
  `chacha20poly1305` called directly in the test; empty passphrase -> `EmptyPassphrase`; another
  passphrase or one changed byte -> `WrongPassphrase` and the key file unchanged; 107 bytes, another
  magic, memory above 1 GiB -> `BadBackup("length"|"magic"|"memory")` in under 50 ms; NFC "é" vs
  "e"+U+0301 restores; restore into an identity with an entry -> `IdentityInUse`, identity unchanged;
  into one without -> the backup's identity.
- AC7 (S14) `restore_head.rs`: restored identity with entries 0..2 made by a first `Log` elsewhere:
  `append` -> `OwnHeadUnconfirmed`; after `ingest` of 0..2 and `confirm_own_head`, the next append
  has seq 3 and prev = id of entry 2; `own_head_confirmed()` is false before and true after.
- AC8 Unsupported `user_version` -> `Storage("unsupported schema version <n>")`, file unchanged.
- AC9 `cargo test -p watane-log -- --include-ignored`, clippy `--all-targets -D warnings`,
  `cargo fmt --check` pass.
