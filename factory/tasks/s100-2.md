## Goal
dev's `fake-claude` can play a turn Alice steers: a long turn that stops when the host writes
`interrupt` and leaves the session usable, a reply for each message she sends, an `ask` that waits
for her answer and goes on (allow) or ends the turn (deny), and a load mode that the stage B budgets
run on (8 sessions, 2 owners, 10 and 10 000 entries). It accepts the launch arguments of `ask: true`.
Exactly as dev TECHNICAL 18.2 now states it (the rows `long-turn`, `load`, `reply-per-say` and the
paragraph after the table, written by the foreman for S100).
## Acceptance
Tests in `dev/tests/it/fake_claude.rs` drive the program over pipes; oracles are literal lines,
and every line the fake writes is parsed with `claude-codes`' `ClaudeOutput` as the existing tests
do (18.1).
AC1. `long-turn {entries: 3, bytes: 16}` writes three `assistant` messages whose texts are exactly
     `entry 1 xxxxxxxx`, `entry 2 xxxxxxxx`, `entry 3 xxxxxxxx` (16 bytes each), then `result`
     (success) and the idle line. Defaults: 100 entries, `every-ms` 0, 32 bytes.
AC2. `long-turn {entries: 1000, every-ms: 5}`; the test writes an `interrupt` after the first entry
     arrives: the fake answers it (`control_response` success with its request id), writes fewer
     than 1000 entries, then `result` with subtype `error_during_execution` and idle; a following
     `expect-user` step accepts the next `user` message (the session goes on).
AC3. `reply-per-say {turns: 2}`: two `user` messages `a` and `b` each get a `delta` and a
     `message` with text `you said: a` / `you said: b`, a `result` and idle; a custom `text` prefix
     is used when given; without `turns` it replies until stdin closes, then exits 0. An `interrupt`
     while it waits is answered and logged, not exit 70.
AC4. `ask {await: true}` then `message {text: "after"}`: an allow answer from the test is logged and
     `after` follows; a deny `{"behavior":"deny","message":"no"}` writes `message` `denied: no`,
     `result` and idle and skips to the next `expect-user` (the `after` step is not written); an
     `interrupt` in place of the answer is answered and ends the turn the same way.
AC5. `load {entries: 10000}` writes 10 000 `assistant` entries of 1024 bytes of text each, then
     `result` and idle, in order, within 5 s on the cloud machine (record the time in the test's
     output, not as an assertion tighter than 5 s).
AC6. Arguments: `--permission-prompts=host --permission-prompt-tool=stdio` are accepted (no exit
     64); an `interrupt` no step awaits is answered and logged, never exit 70; the lines of
     DEV-S-56 (`apply_flag_settings`, `updatedPermissions`, ...) still exit 70.
AC7. dev FUNCTIONAL: one scenario row (DEV-S-66 or the next free number) for AC2–AC4 with
     traces; dev TECHNICAL 15.5's table lists the new tests. `cargo clippy --all-targets
     --all-features -- -D warnings` and `cargo +nightly fmt --check` clean.

  followed by the `interrupt` (host 19.3 step 5): the fake must accept that sequence while an
  awaited ask is open (deny ends the turn; the interrupt that follows is answered).
