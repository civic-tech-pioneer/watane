## Goal
dist stage S7 (step 5). A home can fetch a release of a channel it neither follows nor lists: it
finds where that key publishes in the live `list` and `include` statements of the channels it
already reads (own, the consumer that needs the provider, followed, chosen, listed, in that trust
order), reads the channel from those bases with every limit and the local-address rule of a
listed read, verifies it against the wanted key, and creates no record. `Fetched.link` says which
base served (the most trusted one). `read_link` gives a verified package from a link without
following. `search` filters on `Query.origin` (derived mods). Every step 3 and step 4 test passes
unchanged.
