## Goal

The manager reuses stored channel views across refreshes and tells readers
plainly why a channel could not be read. Install consent names the publishers
of changed linked releases that will be followed.

## Acceptance

1. A mixed plan's follow copy includes the changed unfollowed publisher and
   omits the unchanged unfollowed publisher (S116 P1.1).
2. Remove the unnecessary lint allowance on the home fixture (P1.2). Record
   the extra S116 paths in UNLISTED FILES in the delivery report (P1.3).
3. With an installed published mod, repeating the channel snapshot used by
   mods_list issues zero full channel reads, while a new local head refreshes
   the profile. The periodic channel list also reuses memoized party views.
4. Channel rows present plain words for transport problems, with the
   complete cause available on the Updates card.
