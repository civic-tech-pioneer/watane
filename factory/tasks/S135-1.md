## Goal

After an interrupted install, the person can identify the lock and files left by the interrupted run, recover the install, and uninstall without losing folders they created.

## Acceptance

1. Undo a pre-commit swap using the readable recovery receipt's records and the journal together. A relink to the recorded folder can resume recovery. A different folder and files outside receipt ownership are preserved.
2. Pin the seven S130 recovery guards named in S135 with focused tests that fail when the corresponding guard is removed.
3. After SIGKILL, install first reports the stale lock. Read-only status names it and reports the real destination paths of the files moved at point D. Once the lock is removed and recovery finishes, the script confirms it. Uninstall after a fresh pre-commit kill prunes the empty Watane staging roots.
4. Reinstall drops creation records for relinked roots. The receipt records bin and share roots created by Watane, and uninstall preserves pre-existing roots and removes empty roots it created.
5. Rustup's closing delimiter follows a completed output line without adding a blank line. The install refusal for a destination outside the home reads naturally.
