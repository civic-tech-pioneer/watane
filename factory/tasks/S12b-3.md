## Goal
A mod's page hears only about its own mod. Today, after the host delivers a provider's event to a
subscriber, it also sends the provider's raw `{"type":"event","from":"<provider>",...}` message to
the subscriber's pages (host TECHNICAL 9.2 step 5). That is noise for the page: it cannot call
the provider anyway. After this task a subscriber's pages get its own events, and `changed` when
its state changed (unchanged rule), never another mod's event.
