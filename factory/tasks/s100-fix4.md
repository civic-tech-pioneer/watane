## Goal
The host's asks and checks match the kernel's reading of them and close strictly at their
deadline: `ask-resolved` names the ask by `id`; a file-tool ask shows the path it really reaches;
nothing is asked before `system/init` passed; an answer at or after `expires-at` loses; `answered`
is pushed only once the answer reached `claude`. Review r1 of PR #15, findings F1.1 to F1.5 and
F1.10, as host TECHNICAL 19.3 now states them (commit c11418b).
## Acceptance
AC1 (F1.1). `ask-resolved` items carry `id`, never `ask`: a unit test asserts
     `resolved_item("1","answered",Some("allow"))` parses to exactly
     `{"type":"ask-resolved","id":"1","by":"answered","outcome":"allow"}`; every `claude.rs` test
     that compares an `ask-resolved` uses the new key.
AC2 (F1.2). An ask of `Read` with `file_path` `link.txt`, where `<ws>/link.txt` is a symlink to
     `<ws>/real.txt`, is exactly `{"type":"ask","id":"1","tool_use_id":…,"tool":"Read","input":{"file_path":"link.txt"},"path":"<canonical ws>/real.txt","expires-at":…}`;
     an ask whose path the rule refuses, and an ask of a tool that is not one of the six, has no
     `path` key; absent path gives the canonical working folder.
AC3 (F1.3). A `can_use_tool` and a `hook_callback` written after the `initialize` answer and before
     `system/init` are denied at once (the fake's stdin holds the deny) and push nothing; the
     same after `system/init` passed become items.
AC4 (F1.4). With the test clock: an `answer` called at the instant of `expires-at` (before any
     sweep could run; e.g. the clock moved and `answer` called at once) is `unknown-ask`; an answer
     accepted at the call whose apply comes after the deadline writes no allow (the ask's single
     expiry deny is the only `control_response` for it on stdin); a check: no stdin line.
AC5 (F1.5). `answered` is pushed after the line is written: with the fake's stdin closed before
     the answer applies (e.g. the fake exits after writing the ask), no `ask-resolved answered` is
     pushed for it; `Session::write` no longer ignores a failed enqueue (it reports it to the
     caller or the stream ends).
AC6 (F1.10). The AC2/AC3 tests of `claude.rs` (around lines 1361, 1434, 1518) compare whole
     items and whole written responses (the full `control_response` envelope); add a non-string
     `tool_use_id` case (deny at once, no item) and an `ExitPlanMode` allow with a changed input
     (written as `updatedInput`); the protected-path test advances the test clock past
     `expires-at` and asserts each refused ask gets exactly one deny and `ask-resolved expired`
     (not an interrupt).
AC7. `cargo test -p watane-host` green; clippy -p watane-host and nightly fmt clean; FUNCTIONAL
     scenarios in step with the new key (already edited in c11418b; adjust if needed).
