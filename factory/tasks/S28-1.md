## Goal
Every channel can carry index statements. Ivy's dist signs `list` (a mod link and a note),
`include` (a channel link and a note) and `unlist` (ends one of them), each after exactly one
confirmation, and refuses a bad one before it asks. Every view, own or read, derives Ivy's live
listings with rules 9 to 14, and a read of Ivy's channel reports new listings and unlistings as
changes. This task builds the statements only; choosing an index, read sets and search are S28-2
and S28-3.
