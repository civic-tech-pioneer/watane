## Goal

The forced redb reopen regression exercises the shutdown deadline without
making every net unit test run wait five seconds.

## Acceptance

1. The forced stall waits for a 200 ms unit-test deadline, reports the existing
   database reopen error, and succeeds on retry after the held store closes.
2. Production and integration builds keep the 5 s deadline for close-before-reopen.
