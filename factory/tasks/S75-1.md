## Goal
The app opens its home through `watane_node::Node` and gives Alice her own machines in Settings
(the `#machines` section, which reads "unavailable" since S74): turn them on after the `net_on`
text, a pairing code for a full or light machine with Copy and its expiry, the join question in the
consent view with the machine's short key and role, join with a code (deleting an unused identity
after a question), the list of machines with role, online, last seen and last pull, remove, role
change, expiry, relays and the DHT switch behind their consent texts, the problems list, the
replica identity view, and restore hints after a restore. Every call with a net side goes through
`node`; no mod and no local route reaches `node.net()`. With own machines off everything works as
before. Also S61's leftovers: `Core::indexes` fails on a keyless home (it must show the indexes it
holds); `choose_index` reports `NoIdentity` after it worked (a keyless home chooses an index
without signing anything, or fails before changing anything, never both); the `IdentityInUse`
text says "replaced"/"signed statements" after one (it must read true for one statement too:
fix the text in node's `NodeError` only if node owns it, else in app's mapping). App stage G
(app/TECHNICAL.md 12 row G) except `tests/j8.rs` (S75-2).
