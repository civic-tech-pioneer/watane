## Goal
Alice's main mod opens at start instead of the manager. She can make any mod with a page main and
unset it in its details; a mod that proposes itself (`main = true`) becomes main when she installs
it and none is set. `Mods…` and `Settings…` in the app menu open the main window, now split in a
manager (Library, Find, Updates, details with connections and a warning before disable/remove)
and Settings (identity, site, backup, agents, machines). App stage I (app/TECHNICAL.md 12 row I).
