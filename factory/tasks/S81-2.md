## Goal
Own machines in Settings (app 9.8) say what they mean. This machine's line no longer reads
`anchor · anchor <key>`; the anchor's own row in the machine list does not read `Last seen:
unknown` for the machine you are on; `Join` cannot be clicked twice while a join starts; the relay
and DHT controls are disabled while own machines are off; a publish that would make this home's
identity sign its first statement warns that the home can then no longer join own machines; and
when publishing is refused after a restore because the restored own head is not confirmed (dist
"confirm the restored own head before signing"), the text says where to do that: Settings,
identity, `From your machines` (or restore again).

## Acceptance
- AC1 On the anchor, `#machine-info` reads `This machine: <short> · anchor` (no second
  `anchor`); on a replica `This machine: <short> · <full|light> of <anchor short>`; alone
  `This machine: <short> · alone` (or as the code says it today when alone).
- AC2 The row of the machine you are on (`you`) reads `this machine` and no `Last seen`/
  `online` part; other rows unchanged.
- AC3 After one click on `#join` (signed home, so no question), `#join` is disabled at once, before
  any `machines` refresh; a second click sends no second `machine_join` (driver record or command
  count). It is enabled again when the join fails.
- AC4 With own machines off, `#relay-url`, `#relay-add`, the relay `Remove relay` buttons and
  `#dht-on` are disabled; turning on enables them.
- AC5 The consent view of a publish (Sign) on a home whose identity signed nothing yet
  (`Status.identity.signed == false`) and that is not a replica shows the line `After this, this
  home cannot join your other machines.`; a home that signed shows no such line.
- AC6 A publish refused with dist's unconfirmed-restored-head error shows a text that names
  Settings and `From your machines` (CmdError text and the `/api/publish` error message).
- AC7 Each new text in app/TECHNICAL.md 9.8 (or 6.3 for the consent line); APP-F-54, 55 if their
  facts change.
- AC8 Green: app tests (`cargo test -p watane-app --features driver -- --include-ignored`, the four
  front tests skipped, j8 included), clippy and fmt clean.
