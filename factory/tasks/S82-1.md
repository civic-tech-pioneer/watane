## Goal
A manager mod can call `open-page(local)` in `watane:host/manage`: a direct call that, when the
manager's call succeeds, reaches the app through `Confirm::reduced` as `Reduction::OpenPage(local)`
(the app then opens or focuses that mod's window; that is a later task). It gives no authority. The
manager fixture exports it, and `Harness` fakes and records it like a reduction.

  `close-page`, with the doc comment of host/TECHNICAL 6.4; `HOST_WIT` equals it
  (`tests/runtime.rs`'s literal updated).
- AC2 `manager`'s `api.open-page("shelf")` on a served host: `ok`, and after the call the test
  `Confirm` received `reduced(manager, Reduction::OpenPage(shelf))`, in order with other effects

- AC3 Failures: unknown mod → `no_mod`; a disabled mod, or a mod with no page (no `ui/`) →
