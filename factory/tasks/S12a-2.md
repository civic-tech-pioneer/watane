## Goal
App stage B: the app runs the real host of Alice's home in its process. She starts the app,
chooses the folders of `reading-list` and `recommendations`, sees the plan in the consent view
(install order, "local", grant words, the provider each uses), withholds a grant if she wants,
approves, and uses both pages together. After a restart her data is there. She can withdraw a
grant and give it back, disable, enable and remove a mod (keeping or deleting its data), and
give an agent a token for a toolset. `src/fake.rs` and `WATANE_APP_FAKE` are gone; every window
test runs against the real host. `tests/j2.rs` is journey J2.
