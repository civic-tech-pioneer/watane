## Goal
A maker's agent running `watane-dev test` on a mod whose own outlet holds a collect can fake the
collect with the whole list of answers (or get `[]` with no fake), and in a subscriber mod can
`deliver` a collect and check its answer with `returns`. The template `AGENTS.md` tells the agent
what a collect is and how to fake and answer one (dev stage 12).
