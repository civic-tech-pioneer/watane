## Goal

Make the oversized `/call` regression require an actual 413 and close, and document how
`Served::shutdown` uses its time budget and releases the home. The contract closes an
oversized request without waiting to drain its unread body.

## Acceptance

- AC1. A valid chunked `/call` request larger than the body limit receives a complete 413
  with `connection: close` even though the client did not request close. A declared body
  above the limit gets the same response before sending any body. Neither call runs.
- AC2. `Served::shutdown` documents the 4 s router budget, 5 s overall wait, run file cleanup,
  and the possibility that still-running work retains the home.
