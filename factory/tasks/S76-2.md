## Goal
A mod holding the grant `Manage` (a manager) can, through `watane:host/manage`, read every installed
mod, make things do less at once (disable, withdraw a grant, close a page, each told to the app
first via `Confirm::reduced`), and ask the participant for anything more (install, update, enable,
grant, remove) as a request with an id whose state it can read; the app decides each in its
consent view through `Confirm::manage`. Effects apply only after the manager's chain succeeds, and
the grant is checked again before each. In a `Harness`, the interface is faked and its reductions
and requests are recorded in `Outcome.manages` (host build plan stage 11). Build Q28 to Q33 as
marked "built" (option a) in host/TECHNICAL.md 18.
