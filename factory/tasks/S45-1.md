## Goal
When a plan finds two or more mods that could provide the same pinned provider to a consumer
(possible in a preview, W27), the `ambiguous_provider` error names the consumer, the provider it
needs, and every competing mod by local name, and says that picking one of them settles it. Today
it says only `ambiguous provider: <interface>`.
