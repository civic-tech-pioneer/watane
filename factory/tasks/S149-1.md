## Goal

The host's S147 r1 polish is closed: a chunked oversize body on `/api` and on `/mcp` is pinned
to answer 413 with `connection: close` (behaviour S147 added, untested), and host TECHNICAL 8.1
says plainly what happens on the two paths it is silent about today: `extra_api` routes (an
app's own routes keep their own `DefaultBodyLimit`, axum's refusal, no early 413 and no added
close) and HTTP/2 without TLS (h2c: accepted by `axum::serve`; HTTP/2 has no `Connection`
header and its stream framing keeps an unread body from being read as a request; untested,
since the test crate has no HTTP/2 client; refusing it is a later spec call). No behaviour
changes. Every other S149 item was found already fixed on main (see the PR).

## Acceptance

- AC1. A chunked body over the 64 KiB limit on a built-in `/api/` route (for example
  `/api/plan`), sent without `Connection: close` from the client, gets 413 with response header
  `connection: close`, and a request pipelined after it is not served.
- AC2. The same for a chunked oversize body on `/mcp/<toolset>` with a valid bearer.
- AC3. host TECHNICAL 8.1 carries one sentence on `extra_api` routes and one on h2c, as the
  Goal says; nothing else in 8.1 changes meaning.
