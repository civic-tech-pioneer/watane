## Goal
pkg knows the grant to manage mods (W35): a mod whose world imports `watane:host/manage@0.1.0`
has `World::manage` true and declares the grant `Manage`, which reads and writes as
`{"kind":"manage"}` and the text `manage`, sorts last, and is carried in a release body as the CBOR
`[4]`. Every earlier golden byte is unchanged (pkg build plan stage 11).

## Acceptance
- AC1 (A30) a world importing `watane:host/state@0.1.0` and `watane:host/manage@0.1.0` (with a page
  and an export) is read: `World::manage` is true, grants are exactly `[Storage, Manage]`.
- AC2 `Grant::Manage` <-> `{"kind":"manage"}` and <-> the text `manage`, both ways (literal strings).
- AC3 `Manage` sorts after a `Subscribe` grant; `same_authority(Manage, Manage)` is true.
- AC4 (A31) the release body of the A30 mod encodes its grants as the hex `828100 8104` (written by
  hand) and decodes back to `[Storage, Manage]`; a release whose grants hold `[4]` before a
  `[3, ...]` is refused as not ascending.
- AC5 every existing pkg test and golden byte passes unchanged; `cargo test -p watane-pkg`,
  `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check` clean, and
  `cargo build --workspace --all-targets` compiles (see Files for crates outside pkg).
