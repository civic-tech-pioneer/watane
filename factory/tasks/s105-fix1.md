Slice: S105 — a listed base cannot lead dist into the local network

## Goal

The adversary's breach is closed. Today, when Eve has a listed record for Carol (it came from a
listing: not followed, not an index), and she then follows Carol by Carol's own link
(`follow`), or chooses Carol as an index (`choose_index`), the record keeps the listing's
`base`/`bases` next to the participant's base. Those bases are then read as the participant's own
(`listed = false`), so the redirect check is skipped. After this task, a record that becomes
followed or chosen while it was listed-only holds only the base the participant gave:
`base` = the link's base, `bases` empty. That is what dist/TECHNICAL.md 4 ("holds the bases the
participant gave by links") and 5.9 ("A listing never changes the bases or base of a followed or
chosen channel") already say. A record that was already followed or chosen still learns the new
base as today (DD-41).

Foreman decision (inside dist, cheap to undo): drop the listing bases at the moment a record turns
followed or chosen. The other option, checking redirects on a followed record's listing-learned
bases, would need a per-base origin in `follows.json` (a format change), so it is not taken.
