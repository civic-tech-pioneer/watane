## Goal
A managing mod (grant `manage`, host 6.10) reaches Alice only through the consent view. The app
implements `Confirm::manage` and `Confirm::reduced` on `HostConfirm`: each request of a manager
(install, update, enable, grant, remove) shows in the consent view naming the mod that asks, and the
app acts only after Alice approves; a disable or close-page of a manager closes that mod's window,
and the details of a disabled mod say which mod turned it off until the app restarts. The consent
page shows the new `manage` request and the `Asked by ...` line. This is app stage J (app/TECHNICAL.md
17.2 to 17.4) except the fixture `desk-ui`, `tests/manage.rs` and `tests/j10.rs` (S77-2).
