## Goal
Two things the S28 user-sim found, which the app needs to show plainly. An index's walk now says
which channels it did not read and why (its own channel, a local address, or over the listing
limit), instead of only a count. And dist's error texts name a channel by its 10-character
fingerprint, not its raw 52-character key, so a person can read them.
