## Goal
Carol's main window has a find view. With no index it says "Add an index from a link that
someone shared with you." She pastes Ivy's link and sees Ivy by name, fingerprint, state, "1 mod"
and which channels Ivy's index skipped and why. She searches "reading" and sees Alice's
reading list with its version, summary, Alice's name and fingerprint, "signature verified",
"listed by Ivy" with Ivy's note, and when Alice's channel was last checked. She installs it after
the consent view and Alice becomes a followed channel. A stale result says "could not check since
<time>"; a compromised one says "compromised" and has no install button. From the channels view
she can use a channel as an index, include it in her own index, and list its mods, each through a
signature, and end each listing from her own index row.
