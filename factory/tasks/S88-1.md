## Goal
`j2::ac8_remove_defaults_to_keep_data_and_unknown_keys_fail` (app/tests/it/j2.rs) fails about 1
run in 12, alone and on an idle Mac: `#confirm-panel` is shown but `document.activeElement` is
BODY, not `#confirm-cancel`. The driver's windows are hidden and not key (`document.hasFocus()`
false, `visibilityState` `hidden`). `warn()` (app/ui/app.js) shows the panel and calls
`$('#confirm-cancel').focus()`; nothing in app.js obviously blurs later. A product-owner try that
reopened the panel after a Library redraw did not change the rate and was reverted.
After this task the cause is known and fixed where it lives, and the test passes reliably without
being weakened (app AGENTS: never weaken a test). Find the cause first, with evidence, before any
fix. Candidates to rule in or out, at least:
- a `refresh()` in flight at the click (the install just finished): `modsView` with a new signature
  runs `cancelConfirm(); document.body.append($('#confirm-panel'))`, which detaches a focused
  element (focus goes to BODY), and something reopens or never hides the panel;
- `selectMod`/`warn` focusing `#confirm-cancel` while its section or the panel is not yet
  rendered (WebKit drops `focus()` on an element not being rendered), or a later style change;
- WebKit dropping or resetting focus in a hidden, non-key page (a `focus`/`visibilitychange`
  event, or the driver's eval/window handling in app/src/driver*);
- the test's own order (it clicks right after `installed()`, which only waits for the section).
Instrument (focusin/focusout/blur listeners with `new Error().stack` or a log array read by
`app.eval`, counters on `modsView`/`warn`/`cancelConfirm`) and loop the test until the failing
case shows its trace. Remove the instrumentation afterwards.
