## Goal

A third party's listing cannot make dist read from the participant's own
network through any address form the S105 adversary named: the local ranges of
5.9 grow to cover shared, benchmarking, this-network and site-local addresses
and the NAT64 and 6to4 forms of a local address. Cheap polish from S105 and S107
rides along. In r1 (F1.1) no redirect of any read may leave http or https.

## Acceptance

1. AC1. A listed base or redirect target in 100.64.0.0/10, 198.18.0.0/15,
   0.0.0.0/8, fec0::/10, 64:ff9b:1::/48, or an IPv4-mapped, 64:ff9b::/96 or
   2002::/16 form of a local IPv4 address is refused before any request, as
   `BadUrl`; `64:ff9b::8.8.8.8` still passes.
2. AC2 (r1, F1.1). A redirect target whose scheme is not http or https
   (`socks5`, `socks4`, `socks`, `socks5h`, `socks4a`, `ftp`, ...) is refused
   before any request, for every read, listed or not, as `BadUrl` with the
   original request URL.
3. AC3 (r1, P1.7). A redirect refused as local says "redirects to a local
   address", not that the asked URL is local.
4. AC4. Polish where cheap (PLAN S112 item 5): S105 P5.3, P5.4, P6.1 to P6.3;
   S107 P3.1, P3.2; each with its test where it changes behaviour.
