## Goal
Contract tests reach other mods: a file's `fakes` answer the mod's calls to its providers, and a
`deliver` step runs a subscription export as the host would for an event. And the slice's story
runs as one test on the real programs: `recommendations` is made, `add`s `reading-list`, passes
its contract tests with a fake and a `deliver` step, and `serve` installs both; a book added on
the reading list's page reaches `recommendations` through its call and its subscription.
