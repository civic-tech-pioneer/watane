Slice: S141 — Make interrupted installs easier to diagnose
## Goal
A person can tell why recovery stopped, which entries are missing during an interrupted upgrade, and whether a receipt is absent or unrecognised.
## Acceptance
1. Uninstall warns if interrupted files may remain after recovery skipped an entry. Relinked entries stay untouched.
2. Read-only status warns about skipped staging files without recommending that the person run an installer through an untrusted folder.
3. After a killed upgrade, status marks a missing destination and reports its backup as a leftover.
4. A folder linked to HOME itself gets a hint to use a real subfolder of HOME.
5. Status and uninstall distinguish an unrecognised receipt from an absent one, keep exit 7, and leave the rejected receipt untouched.
